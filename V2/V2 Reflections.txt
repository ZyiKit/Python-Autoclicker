The auto clicker is now a lot more stable and can run accurately according to 
user's input up to around 150 cps, that will be sufficient for this product.

The cps tester is built since I want a method to collect data regarding the clicks
from auto clicker to measure variation and movement in the future.

A problem is that the cps tester is not very accurate when measuring own input, even less
accurate than a website's cps tester.

In the future I will try to develop a cps tester that plots data directly in an excel sheet
to get that data directly, hopefully this is practical.
